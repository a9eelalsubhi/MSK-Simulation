    Sources & model accuracy

    Study content comes from Upper Limb Vessels — Dr Ibrahim Amr, printed pages 41–51. All displayed lecture images come from the newly supplied L4-Upper Limb Blood Vessels.pdf; page numbers refer to that PDF.

    The 3D model is from Z-Anatomy / BodyParts3D, exported for the web by Anatria3D. It shows general educational anatomy. Original node positions and mesh shapes are preserved. Bone regions, vessel parts and attachment patches are educational approximations; source diagrams provide the finer anatomical detail. Structures with no independent mesh use dotted callouts to related anatomy and the original lecture figures.

    Choose a lecture to load its anatomy and study tools. Model names and lecture terminology are linked in the directory and quiz. Original lecture images remain available as source references.

    Muscle actions use the original static muscle shapes with an illustrative procedural joint rig. Upper-limb actions articulate the shoulder, elbow, radioulnar, wrist and finger joints. Knee tendons follow the patella and tibia; joint pivots and soft-tissue deformation are estimated, not a validated biomechanics simulation. Shared source meshes do not resolve individual fibers or heads. Actions and attachments were checked against the UAMS lower limb muscle table and UAMS upper limb muscle table; the rig follows the segment/path principles described in OpenSim documentation.
