## Geometry and presentation

Original source transforms retained. Lecture 7 now loads 226 meshes, including 91 separately modeled articular structures (312,880 added bytes). Labrum, major pelvic/hip/knee/ankle ligaments and menisci are rendered directly. Hip-region tinting and nearest-surface landmark patches remain approximate teaching overlays. Each lesson isolates its own structures; context and bone-only views are optional. Source figures explain unmodeled details (including bursae); the male asset is never presented as a female mesh.



## Shared features

Full lecture explorer, per-layer switches and opacity, camera presets, search, quiz, touch locking, and source slides remain available. Lecture switching reloads the document and disposes the current scene.



## Visual lab

Pelvis comparison; hip restraints; selectable 3D arch routes and conceptual height comparison; knee cruciate attachment map and meniscus comparison; tibiofibular comparison; ankle movement direction slider; foot joint table. Six connection recall prompts supplement eight functional 3D quiz prompts.

## Visual repair validation
Actual model-name contracts cover acetabulum isolation, hip/pelvic ligaments, menisci, cruciates and ankle ligaments. Local browser inspection checks visibility and camera framing, in addition to shared interaction regression tests.
