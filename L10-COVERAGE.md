## Visual teaching

- 14 chapters plus Full Lecture Explorer; 65 scenes; 39 original nerve meshes; 10 linked nerve profiles; 5 guided routes; 22 genuine identification targets and 12 lecture-derived functional questions.
- Psoas exit sorting (anterior 1 / lateral 4 / medial 2); lumbar and sacral branch circuits with only supplied root values; colored nerve-to-muscle scenes; original skin maps and plantar toe-count schematic; paired deficit reveal; sciatic spared saphenous strip.
- Regional skeleton remains visible at 42% by default. Independent five-layer switches and opacity, search, camera presets, touch lock, quiz and source-reference modal are retained. Changing lecture unloads the previous scene. Muscular assets reuse Lecture 9 rather than duplicating them.
- New geometry is repackaged from Anatria / Z-Anatomy with the original transforms, Draco data and attribution. No invented fine nerves, nerve territories or posture deformations. Unmodeled branches have explicit dotted context pins and original references; they are excluded from identification quizzes.
- Magnus is one intact mesh: adductor part is distinguished from hamstring part by the explanation. Lumbricals are one grouped mesh: first lumbrical versus the remainder is stated explicitly.
- Lecture-specific statements are retained: brevis in both obturator divisions; lateral cutaneous thigh territory as listed; sciatic injury 90% common peroneal-part statement. Injury effects are lecture summaries, not predictions for every lesion level.
- Plantar illustration on printed p.65 shows nerve routes. The toe-count chart is a labeled schematic of the written 3½ / 1½ supply; it is not a new measured skin boundary. The congenital clubfoot illustration on p.69 explains the named posture rather than simulating all acquired nerve injuries.
